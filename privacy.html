Privacy Policy

Lotto Quant Media Uploader · Last updated 30 September 2026

Who we are

Lotto Quant Media Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated privately by the owner of the Golden West channels to publish to YouTube channels he owns. Contact: leveretteh@gmail.com.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests the YouTube scopes youtube.upload and youtube. With them it reads and updates the authorised channel's own data: channel identity and branding, the channel's videos and their titles, descriptions, privacy status, schedules, statistics and playlists, and it uploads new videos and thumbnails.

How the data is used

Solely to publish and manage the owner's own content and to measure how that content performs. The app does not access other users' data, does not read private messages, and does not use the data for advertising.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and working data are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a folder readable only by the owner's user account. Nothing is stored on third-party servers by the app.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone. No data is transferred to third parties except to Google/YouTube itself when publishing.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. On revocation, or on request to the contact address above, locally stored tokens are deleted.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
